How it works

Three steps. No plugin marketplace.

Nothing to install, nothing to provision by hand. The widget, the transport, and the console are all part of the same product.

01

Add the script

One tag, dropped anywhere in your page. It loads a small widget, isolated from the rest of your site.

<script src="https://chat.reserve-me.ru/widget.js" data-site="your-site-id" async></script>
02

Visitors start talking

A bubble appears in the corner. Messages arrive in real time — no page reloads, no polling delay.

03

Your team answers

Operators work from one console: conversation history, file exchange, and presence, kept in sync across every open tab.

Under the hood

Built like it has to stay up.

AGO Chat runs on its own infrastructure. No black-box SaaS behind it, no shared multi-tenant shortcuts — the isolation between Shop One and Shop Two above is real, not a demo trick.

⚡

Real-time delivery

Messages move over a live connection, not a polling loop — replies land while you're still looking at the screen.

▦

Multi-tenant by design

Every site is its own tenant, enforced end to end — not a filter applied after the fact.

↺

Reconnects don't lose messages

Drop your connection mid-conversation and reconnect — nothing already sent goes missing.

📎

Attachments included

Files move through direct, presigned uploads — never proxied through the chat servers themselves.
