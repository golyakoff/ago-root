What it does not do

A Panel cannot collapse, and has no footer. A settings screen that stacks four Panels is four boxes always open. The Save button sits in the form after the last Panel, not in a footer.
A Panel has no error state of its own. A load failure renders an Alert tone="danger" outside the Panel, and the Panel then either shows a skeleton forever or is not rendered at all, depending on the screen. Two screens do this two ways.
