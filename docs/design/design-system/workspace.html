Three regions, two of which are fixed tracks

grid-template-columns: 21rem minmax(0, 1fr) 18rem. 21rem is the narrowest the rail can be while a visitor id badge, an unread badge and “Waiting 14m” still sit on two comfortable lines; 18rem is the aside’s equivalent for a full uuid at 12px mono. The middle is minmax(0, 1fr) rather than 1fr, because a grid track’s default min-width: auto would let one long unbroken message push the layout wider than the viewport.

The stylesheet states its own posture, and it is worth quoting because it is a product decision rather than a style one: “This is a laptop tool that degrades honestly, not a mobile app: the goal at the narrow end is ‘usable and not broken’, not ‘designed for a phone’.”

Queue rows — and the difference between a link and a fact

An assigned row is a whole-row link and looks like one. A waiting row is read-only by design, so it is visibly not a control: no anchor, no hover response, no pointer, a flat sunken surface and a dashed edge that reads as “information”, not “target”.

The waiting row’s appearance is load-bearing and reflects a product fact: there is no claim or assign action anywhere in the product. Waiting conversations appear in three places and are actionable in none.

day separator · author named in words on the first message of a run · the corner pointing at its own side is squared off on that message only

Two-tone by author, and the side is never the only signal: the author is stated in words on the first message of every run, which is also what a screen reader reads before it. Bubbles cap at min(46rem, 82%), 92% below 52.5rem.

There is no delivery state on a message. An operator bubble carries a timestamp and nothing else — no sent, delivered, read or failed marker. Failure is reported once, for the whole composer, as an Alert above it.
The thread has no empty state. A conversation with no messages renders an empty <ol>.
The visitor’s end of the same conversation is timestamped differently. The widget shows no timestamps, no day separators and no author labels at all.

Six sections share this shape: previous conversations, tags, notes, outcome, linked channels, unverified contact details. All six are gated on conversation:read and render nothing at all without it — an operator lacking that grant sees the facts block, then a silent gap, then the closing note.
